Delete Your BeatTag Account

BeatTag users can request account deletion directly from the BeatTag app while signed in.

How to delete your account

  1. Open BeatTag and sign in to your account.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Go to the Danger Zone.
  5. Select Delete Account.
  6. Read the confirmation message and confirm the deletion request.

What happens to your data?

Your deletion request is associated with your authenticated BeatTag account. BeatTag will process the account deletion/deactivation and associated account data according to its Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.

Before confirming, make sure you no longer need access to the account or content associated with it.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or the in-app deletion option, use the support/contact information provided on the official BeatTag website to request assistance.
